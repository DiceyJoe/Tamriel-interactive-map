Dawn Era

Creation of Mundus & Convention

Lorkhan convinces the Aedra to create the mortal plane. Magnus flees to Aetherius, tearing the sun and stars into the sky, while the Adamantine Tower anchors linear time.

Merethic Era

Aldmeri Settlement & Return of Ysgramor

Elves migrate from Aldmeris to Summerset and mainland Tamriel. Atmorans led by Ysgramor settle Skyrim, sparking the Dragon War and the fall of the Snow Elves.

First Era (1E 0 – 1E 2920)

Alessian Empire & Disappearance of the Dwemer

Saint Alessia overthrows the Ayleids in Cyrodiil. In 1E 700, the Battle of Red Mountain results in the sudden vanishing of the entire Dwemer race and the rise of the Tribunal.

Second Era (2E 0 – 2E 896)

The Interregnum & Tiber Septim's Conquests

Following the fall of the Akaviri Potentate, Tamriel fractures into the Three Banners War. Centuries later, Tiber Septim uses the Numidium to unite all of Tamriel under the Third Empire.

Third Era (3E 0 – 3E 433)

The Septim Dynasty & The Oblivion Crisis

433 years of Imperial rule marked by the Imperial Simulacrum, the Warp in the West, the awakening of Dagoth Ur, and Mehrunes Dagon's invasion—ended by Martin Septim's sacrifice.

Fourth Era (4E 0 – Present)

The Red Year, The Great War & Return of Alduin

Vvardenfell is devastated by Red Mountain's eruption. The Aldmeri Dominion forces the White-Gold Concordat upon the Empire, while Alduin returns to Skyrim in 4E 201.
